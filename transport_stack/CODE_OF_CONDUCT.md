# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

Contributors, maintainers, and users are expected to communicate respectfully,
focus feedback on the work, protect private operational details, and avoid
harassment, discrimination, or abusive conduct.

## Safety and security

Do not post private keys, Pluto network addresses, personal data, captured
traffic, or exploit details in public discussions. Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)
for security-sensitive reports.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when conduct
violates these standards. Report concerns privately to a maintainer.
